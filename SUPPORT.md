# Support & SLAs

## General Support
For product or usage questions, contact: support@projectnavi.ai

## Legal
For legal questions: legal@projectnavi.ai. Every repository is open source under MIT or
Apache-2.0; its LICENSE file says which.

## Bug Reports & Feature Requests
- File an issue on the appropriate GitHub repository.
- For sensitive issues, email security@projectnavi.ai.

## Security Issues
See [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) for vulnerability reporting.

## Hours and Response
- **General support:** Mon–Fri 09:00–17:00 UTC — We aim to respond within 2 business days.
- **Security issues:** Acknowledged within 48 hours.
